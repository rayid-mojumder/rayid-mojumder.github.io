## Overview

This project investigated **soft organic synaptic transistors** for neuromorphic-device applications. The work was carried out at Penn State and involved hands-on device fabrication and electrical/optical characterization.

## Research Activities

- Fabrication of flexible organic transistor structures
- P3HT semiconductor processing and spin coating
- Electrical characterization using semiconductor measurement systems
- Optical microscopy and device inspection
- Investigation of transistor behavior relevant to artificial-synapse operation

## Tools

Spin coater · Keithley semiconductor measurement system · Optical microscopy · Organic semiconductor processing
